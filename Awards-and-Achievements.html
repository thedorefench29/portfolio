BSIT and BSBA International Student Mobility Program

Why stay inside the classroom when learning can take you across the world?

The BSIT and BSBA students of St. Dominic College of Asia experienced an incredible International Student Mobility Program in Hong Kong–Shenzhen, China, combining education, technology, culture, and adventure in one unforgettable journey.

Highlights of the Global Experience

  • Fun and unforgettable moments at Hong Kong Disneyland
  • Industry visit to High Great, the world-famous drone company featured in the Guinness Book of Records
  • Research presentation at Shenzhen City Polytechnic
  • International exposure, cultural immersion, and global networking opportunities

At SDCA, education goes beyond lectures. We prepare students to become globally competitive professionals ready for the international stage.
