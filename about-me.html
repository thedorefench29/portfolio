About Me

Get to Know Me

Hello! I am a Information Technology. As a Deaf individual, I have learned the importance of determination, patience, and finding effective ways to communicate and collaborate with others.

During my studies, I developed an interest in web development, programming, and software creation. I enjoy learning new technologies, solving coding problems, debugging applications, and improving my projects.

I am passionate about building user-friendly, accessible, and functional websites. I believe that being Deaf does not define the limits of my potential. My Story

From a quiet world to a future in IT

Living with Waardenburg syndrome has shaped how I experience the world. It gave me distinctive eye pigmentation and hearing loss, and with a cochlear implant I learned to hear, communicate, and speak through patience, extra effort, and hard work. Information Technology was not my original childhood dream. My path into IT began when a cochlear implant, the first high-tech device to transform my daily life, opened the world of sound and speech to me.

That experience made technology personal. It sparked my curiosity about how devices, audio signals, and software work together, and showed me how technology can make communication and connection more accessible in everyday life. It is a perspective I carry with me as I learn and build in IT.

Recognizing both the high financial cost and profound impact of this life-changing technology sparked a deep curiosity in me. I wanted to understand how technology works from the inside out, and how hardware components, audio signals, and software systems interact to solve real human challenges. What began as a personal need to navigate a quiet world gradually grew into a lifelong drive to master technology. Living with Waardenburg syndrome taught me that barriers can be overcome and that persistence leads to progress.

I bring that same discipline, grit, and problem-solving mindset to every technical challenge I take on. Experiencing firsthand how advanced technology can change a person's life gave me a unique perspective: IT is not just about code or circuits, but about building meaningful solutions that empower people. As I prepare for my upcoming IT internship, I am eager to bring my work ethic, technical skills, and adaptability into a professional environment, continuing to turn challenges into strength and growth.
